**Why it fits:**  
This is the best technical match in today's batch. The role is pure production ML — predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and asset health monitoring for one of the world's top-10 copper producers. The requirements map almost directly onto Sheila's background: Python ✓, XGBoost ✓, scikit-learn ✓, time-series forecasting (ARIMA, Prophet, LSTM) ✓, SQL ✓, MLOps (MLflow/Databricks) ✓. MSc/PhD in Mathematics listed as a nice-to-have ✓. The work is building real production ML pipelines for sensor/telemetry/IoT data — not analytics or dashboards.

**Gaps / Concerns:**  
- **Consulting/outsourcing firm**: N-iX is a Ukrainian IT outsourcing company. The actual client is interesting (major copper producer), but Sheila would be employed by N-iX, not the client. This typically means lower IC autonomy, more process overhead, and a delivery-first culture. This is the primary reason for the interest penalty.  
- **Remote policy ambiguous**: "Office/Remote, Europe, Ukraine" is not explicit about whether fully remote from Spain is accepted. N-iX has offices in Ukraine, Poland, Romania, Bulgaria, and Portugal (among others). A Spain-based candidate may be accepted as EU remote, or they may require proximity to one of their Eastern European offices. Must clarify before investing heavily.  
- **Azure/Databricks vs AWS/SageMaker**: Minor stack gap — Sheila's MLOps experience is AWS-based. Not a dealbreaker given transferable concepts.  
- **No salary listed**: Cannot screen on salary. Consulting firms in Eastern European markets often pay below Western European rates even for EU candidates; worth probing in screening.

**Key requirements:**  
5+ years ML/DS experience; Python + ML stack (scikit-learn, XGBoost, time-series); Azure ML / Databricks / MLflow or similar; SQL; production ML deployment experience; IoT/telemetry/sensor data is a plus.

**Remote policy:**  
"Office/Remote" in N-iX's framing usually means hybrid for candidates near their offices, or fully remote for EU-based candidates in countries they hire in. Spain is in the EU but N-iX doesn't prominently list a Spain office — email/clarify remote flexibility before applying. If EU remote is confirmed, this becomes more interesting.

**Verdict:**  
Apply cautiously — strong technical fit, but the consulting model and remote ambiguity are real concerns. If Sheila is open to consulting work and remote from Spain is confirmed, this is worth a first-round call to probe compensation and IC structure. **Low-to-medium priority.** Don't skip if the week is light.
