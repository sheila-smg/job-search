The domain (trading cards) is non-standard but the ML problem structure (illiquid asset valuation under sparse data) is highly analogous to insurance pricing at Justos and prediction market modeling at Polymarket. PyTorch/TF is listed but alongside scikit-learn, and pricing models here will be primarily tree-based/gradient-based.

**Key requirements:**
- 5+ years DS experience with feature engineering and model-building in pricing/financial modeling/marketplace dynamics ✓
- Expert-level SQL, Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost/LightGBM, PyTorch/TF ✓ (PyTorch secondary)
- Experimental design, backtesting, sparse/noisy data ✓

**Compensation:** Listed as $200,000 — well above the EUR floor regardless of currency conversion (~180K EUR). No issues on salary.

However, the posting does not explicitly say "US Remote" or restrict to US states, and small startups occasionally hire contractors internationally. **Sheila should apply and immediately ask whether EU-based candidates are considered.** This is worth a quick inquiry before investing significant application effort.

**Verdict:** Top priority — perfect skills match. Send an exploratory message or apply immediately with a note about EU-based remote. The role is too aligned to skip on a location uncertainty alone.
